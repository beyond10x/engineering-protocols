story:software-change-profiles — its scope and acceptance leave the generator's refusal on case inputs in place and name nobody who lifts it, so `task check` cannot pass on this story. Its body must either own the graph work or point to a story that does. That work is: emit the `input` leaf and `applicable_when` in `graph::document`, update the schema transcription, and move the docs-system pin in `website/package.json`. It also needs an acceptance line for the emitted graph — `.engineering/planning/story/software-change-profiles.md:104`, `.engineering/planning/story/canon-0-2-0-pin.md:42`, `.engineering/planning/blocker/graph-schema-case-inputs.md:21`

The blocker's "Clears when" asks for a docs-system commit, a rendering component and a pin bump (blocker line 21). That clears the external side only.
- **What is unowned.** The profiles scope lists `website/data/protocol-graphs/software-change-1.json` as generated (profiles line 118). It lists no `crates/canon-engineering-docs/src/graph.rs`, no `crates/canon-engineering-docs/tests/review_graph_schema.rs` and no `website/package.json`. A grep for `graph.rs`, `package.json` and `review_graph_schema` in `software-change-profiles.md` finds nothing.

- **What closes it.** Profiles gets that work in scope plus an acceptance line, or a separate story takes it, with a `depends_on` edge from profiles to that story and the blocker cleared first.
