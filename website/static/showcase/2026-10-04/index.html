01 / Replay

Who did what, when.

One lane per participant. Colour is the protocol the work belonged to. Press play or drag the scrubber: the lanes fill in as the night advances, and the composition graph in section 06 lights up as each protocol gets its first case.

02 / Concurrency

How many agent participants had a recorded episode running at each second: the two sessions and the sub-agents. The operator and the pull requests are shown as separate strips on the same time axis, not added in.

03 / Where the time went

Each participant's observed span, split by kind of time. Sub-agents are counted from their first launch to their last report; the others across the whole window. Where two kinds overlap in one lane, repair beats deciding beats working beats waiting.

04 / Decision anatomy

The hand-off takes seconds. The record waits.

05 / What if

Four protocol changes, priced on this night.

The four changes proposed in the process-timings note, section 5. Each switch recomputes its estimate from the data and redraws sections 01–04. Model estimate marks every number here: it is what the recorded timestamps imply under a stated assumption, not a measurement of a night that happened.

Not added together.

The four estimates measure different clocks: record latency summed per record, decision latency, agent working time, and repair time. Adding them would count overlapping wall-clock time more than once.

06 / Composition

The composition behind it.

Triggers open protocols; protocols are taken by agents and principals; agents act through capabilities; capabilities bind to connections. Hover or focus a node to trace everything upstream and downstream of it. During the replay, a node is lit once a protocol that reaches it has had its first case.
